Atelier Ishimura

Leather, slowly.
Stitched by hand. ゆっくりと、手縫いで

A small workshop devoted to the patient craft of full-grain leather — wallets, bags, and journals shaped one stitch at a time, made to soften and deepen with the years.

Philosophy

Three things we return to, again. 考え方

Vegetable-tanned

Hides tanned with bark and oak — slower, kinder, and built for a long second life.

Saddle-stitched

Every seam sewn by hand with waxed linen. If one stitch fails, the rest hold.

Unmarked

No logos pressed into the grain. The maker's mark sits inside, like a signature.

Repaired, not replaced

Every piece returns to the bench for the cost of materials. Built to outlast.
